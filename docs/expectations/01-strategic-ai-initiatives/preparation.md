# 3. Preparation guide: Topics to study and reference links

> Part of Expectation 1: Lead architecture for strategic AI initiatives across products, platforms, and shared enterprise capabilities

For an AI Principal Architect interview, focus on architecture decisions, enterprise-scale design, strategic alignment, governance, and the ability to explain trade-offs.

### A. Enterprise AI architecture and reference architectures

| **Topic** | **What to prepare** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise AI architecture | AI architecture layers, capabilities, interoperability, reusable services | [AI Reference Architecture – Australian Government](https://www.digital.gov.au/ai-reference-architecture?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Agentic AI architecture | Agent orchestration, model access, tools, knowledge, observability | [AWS Enterprise Agentic AI Architecture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/govern-architect-agentic-ai/enterprise-architecture.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| AI-native enterprise architecture | AI agents, business processes, enterprise platforms | [SAP AI-Native North Star Architecture](https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ai-native-north-star-architecture?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Multi-agent architecture | Multi-tenancy, security, centralized governance, business-unit isolation | [Google Cloud Multi-Tenant Agentic AI Architecture](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/multi-tenant-agentic-ai-system?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

Interview preparation questions:

1. How would you define an enterprise AI reference architecture?
1. How would you prevent individual business units from developing isolated AI solutions?
1. What capabilities should be centralized versus decentralized?
1. How would you design an enterprise AI platform that supports multiple products?

### B. AI platform architecture and reusable capabilities

Prepare the following topics:

- Enterprise AI platform architecture.
- LLM gateway and model routing.
- RAG architecture and enterprise search.
- Agent orchestration and tool calling.
- AI integration with BPM and legacy applications.
- Model lifecycle management.
- AI evaluation and observability.
- Cloud AI services and infrastructure.

#### Recommended resources:

- [AWS – Building an Enterprise-Ready Generative AI Platform](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/prescriptive-guidance/latest/strategy-enterprise-ready-gen-ai-platform/strategy-enterprise-ready-gen-ai-platform.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- [AWS – Enterprise Agentic AI Architecture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/govern-architect-agentic-ai/enterprise-architecture.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Interview preparation questions:

1. How would you design a shared AI platform for 20 business applications?
1. How would you design an enterprise RAG capability that respects document-level access permissions?
1. How would you integrate AI agents with existing enterprise workflow systems?
1. How would you balance centralized AI governance with product-team autonomy?

### C. AI governance, security, and responsible AI

Prepare:

- Responsible AI principles.
- AI risk classification.
- Model evaluation and validation.
- Data privacy and security.
- Explainability and traceability.
- Human-in-the-loop controls.
- AI lifecycle governance.
- Production monitoring and incident management.

#### Recommended resource:

[AI Reference Architecture – Governance, Assurance and Human Oversight](https://www.digital.gov.au/ai-reference-architecture/overview?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The reference architecture includes trustworthiness and assurance capabilities such as security, privacy, fairness, explainability, traceability, and resilience.

digital.gov.au+1

Interview preparation questions:

1. How would you govern AI initiatives across multiple business units?
1. How would you architect a responsible AI solution for a regulated industry?
1. What architectural controls would you introduce to prevent unauthorized agent actions?
1. How would you balance innovation, compliance, and speed of delivery?

### D. Enterprise architecture and strategic leadership

Since this is a Principal Architect position, prepare the broader enterprise architecture discipline alongside AI technology.

| **Topic** | **Preparation focus** |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise architecture | Business, application, data, and technology architecture |
| Architecture strategy | Business alignment, strategic capabilities, investment priorities |
| Architecture governance | Design authority, architecture reviews, decision records |
| Transformation roadmaps | Current state, target state, capability gaps, phased implementation |
| Technology strategy | Build vs. buy, vendor evaluation, platform selection |
| Architecture leadership | Stakeholder alignment, team enablement, conflict resolution |

#### Recommended resources:

- [AI Reference Architecture – Enterprise Architecture Perspective](https://www.digital.gov.au/ai-reference-architecture?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- [AI4EA – Artificial Intelligence for Enterprise Adoption](https://ai4ea.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The AI4EA framework is particularly useful for connecting AI strategy, architecture, governance, scaling, and business value measurement.
