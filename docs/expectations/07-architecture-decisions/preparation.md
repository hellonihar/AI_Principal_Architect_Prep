# 12. Interview questions to prepare

> Part of Expectation 7: Guide architecture decisions across application, data, integration, platform, and operational concerns to avoid fragmented or duplicative solutions

1. How would you prevent different business units from building duplicate AI capabilities?
1. How would you decide whether an AI capability should be centralised or owned by an individual business unit?
1. How would you establish an enterprise AI reference architecture?
1. How would you decide whether to reuse an existing vector database or introduce a new one?
1. How would you ensure AI agents integrate securely with enterprise applications?
1. How would you separate AI reasoning from business rules and workflow execution?
1. How would you manage different model providers across enterprise AI applications?
1. How would you balance architecture standardisation with delivery-team autonomy?
1. How would you identify and manage architectural exceptions?
1. How would you measure whether enterprise architecture governance is delivering value?

## 14. Seven-day interview preparation plan

| **Day** | **Preparation topic** | **Practical exercise** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Enterprise architecture principles | Define 10 principles for enterprise AI architecture. |
| 2 | Application architecture | Design a reusable AI application architecture for a bank. |
| 3 | Data architecture | Design a shared enterprise RAG architecture with domain-level access control. |
| 4 | Integration architecture | Design secure API and event integration for an AI agent. |
| 5 | Platform architecture | Design an enterprise AI platform supporting multiple model providers. |
| 6 | Operational architecture | Define observability, resilience, deployment and cost-management standards. |
| 7 | Architecture governance | Present the banking case study and defend your architectural decisions. |
