# 6. Strong interview closing statement

> Part of Expectation 3: Define and evolve architecture standards, reference patterns, and guardrails for AI solutions and AI-enabled software development

Use this as your final summary when discussing the expectation:

My approach to AI architecture standards is to establish a strong but flexible architectural foundation that enables enterprise-wide AI adoption without restricting innovation. I define reusable reference architectures, design patterns, and engineering guardrails covering AI applications, RAG, agentic AI, model integration, security, governance, and operational excellence. I work closely with enterprise architecture, AI engineering, security, and product teams to ensure these standards are practical, implementable, and aligned with business priorities. I embed architecture controls into the software development lifecycle through reusable templates, automated validation, architecture reviews, and continuous evaluation. Most importantly, I continuously evolve these standards based on emerging technologies, engineering feedback, production experience, and business requirements to ensure the enterprise can scale AI adoption securely, consistently, and sustainably.
