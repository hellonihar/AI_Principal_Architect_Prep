# Expectation 8: Participate in architecture reviews, technical strategy discussions, and investment decisions related to AI and platform modernization

The important difference between a Solution Architect and a Principal Architect is that the latter must be able to defend decisions at three levels:

1. Technical: Why is this the right architecture?
1. Strategic: Why is this the right technology direction for the enterprise?
1. Financial: Why should the organisation invest in this rather than another opportunity?

Below are five realistic scenarios, the decision frameworks you should use, and examples of how to defend your recommendations.
