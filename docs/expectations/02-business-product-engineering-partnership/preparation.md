# 3. Preparation guide with learning resources

> Part of Expectation 2: Partner with business units, product leaders, and engineering teams to shape scalable, secure, and practical AI solution architectures

For interview preparation, focus on being able to explain how you collaborate, what architectural decisions you make, why you make them, and how you balance business and technical constraints.

### A. Business and product collaboration

| **Topic** | **What to prepare** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business capability mapping | Map business objectives to AI capabilities | [TOGAF Enterprise Architecture](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| AI use-case identification | Business value, feasibility, risk, and prioritization | [Google Cloud Architecture Center](https://cloud.google.com/architecture?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Product architecture | Product boundaries, APIs, capabilities, and integration | [Azure Architecture Center](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| AI product lifecycle | AI product discovery, implementation, evaluation, and monitoring | [Azure AI Architecture Patterns](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/architecture-pattern?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

Questions to prepare:

1. How do you identify AI opportunities with business stakeholders?
1. How do you translate business requirements into an AI solution architecture?
1. How do you resolve disagreements between product priorities and architecture constraints?
1. How do you prioritize AI use cases based on business value and feasibility?

### B. Scalable AI architecture

| **Topic** | **What to prepare** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI architecture patterns | AI application layers, model access, retrieval, and orchestration | [Microsoft Azure AI Architecture Patterns](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/architecture-pattern?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Enterprise AI platforms | Shared AI capabilities and reusable services | [AWS Enterprise-Ready GenAI Platform](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/strategy-enterprise-ready-gen-ai-platform/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Cloud scalability | Reliability, performance, availability, and cost | [Azure Well-Architected Framework](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/framework/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| AI and ML architecture | AI workload scalability and operational design | [Google Cloud AI/ML Well-Architected Perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

Questions to prepare:

1. How would you design an AI platform that supports multiple business units?
1. How would you scale a RAG application to support millions of users?
1. How would you design an AI architecture that supports multiple models and providers?
1. How would you manage performance, latency, and inference costs?

### C. Secure AI architecture

| **Topic** | **What to prepare** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI risk management | AI risk identification, assessment, and mitigation | [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Secure AI workloads | Identity, data protection, network security, and model security | [Azure AI Security Design Principles](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/design-principles?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Responsible AI | Fairness, transparency, explainability, and accountability | [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |
| Agent security | Tool permissions, human approval, and action validation | [Azure AI Architecture and Security](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/architecture-pattern?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

Questions to prepare:

1. How would you secure an enterprise RAG application?
1. How would you prevent an AI agent from performing unauthorized business transactions?
1. How would you protect sensitive data in prompts, embeddings, and logs?
1. How would you implement responsible AI governance across business units?

### D. Engineering collaboration and implementation

| **Topic** | **What to prepare** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| API architecture | REST APIs, API contracts, integration patterns | [Microsoft API Design Best Practices](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/best-practices/api-design?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

| Production readiness | Reliability, deployment, observability, and operational excellence | [AWS Enterprise GenAI Architecture](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/strategy-enterprise-ready-gen-ai-platform/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) |

Questions to prepare:

1. How do you ensure that architecture decisions are implementable by engineering teams?
1. How do you handle disagreements between architects and developers?
1. How do you manage technical debt while delivering AI initiatives?
1. How do you move an AI prototype into production?
