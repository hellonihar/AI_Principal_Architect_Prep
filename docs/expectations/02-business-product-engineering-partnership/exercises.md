# 4. Practical interview preparation exercise

> Part of Expectation 2: Partner with business units, product leaders, and engineering teams to shape scalable, secure, and practical AI solution architectures

Prepare one reusable architecture case study that demonstrates collaboration across business, product, engineering, security, and enterprise architecture.

Case study: Enterprise AI platform for a banking organization

The bank wants to introduce AI capabilities across customer service, loan underwriting, cross-selling, and fraud monitoring.

Prepare to explain:

| **Area** | **What you should demonstrate** |
| --- | --- |
| Business collaboration | How you identify business problems and define measurable outcomes |
| Product collaboration | How you prioritize AI capabilities and define product boundaries |
| Engineering collaboration | How you translate architecture into implementation-ready designs |
| Scalability | How shared AI capabilities support multiple business applications |
| Security | How identity, data protection, and AI risk controls are implemented |
| Practicality | How you reuse existing banking systems and avoid unnecessary complexity |
| Governance | How you establish standards, architecture reviews, and decision processes |
| Business outcomes | How you measure revenue, productivity, customer experience, and operational efficiency |
