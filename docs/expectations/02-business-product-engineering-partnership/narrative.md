# 1. Two-paragraph interview narrative

> Part of Expectation 2: Partner with business units, product leaders, and engineering teams to shape scalable, secure, and practical AI solution architectures

As an AI Principal Architect, I partner closely with business units, product leaders, enterprise architects, data teams, and engineering organizations to translate business objectives into practical, scalable, and secure AI solution architectures. I begin by understanding business challenges, operational constraints, customer expectations, and strategic priorities, and then identify where AI, Generative AI, agentic AI, machine learning, and intelligent automation can deliver measurable value. I facilitate cross-functional discovery workshops, challenge assumptions, evaluate technical feasibility, and define solution architectures that balance business value, technical complexity, integration requirements, and enterprise standards. My focus is on ensuring that AI solutions are not developed as isolated technology experiments but are embedded into business products, processes, and enterprise platforms.

I also work collaboratively with product and engineering teams to translate architectural vision into implementable designs, defining system boundaries, API contracts, data flows, integration patterns, model selection, security controls, and non-functional requirements. I promote reusable architecture patterns, modular design, and shared AI capabilities while allowing product teams sufficient flexibility to innovate. I ensure that AI solutions address enterprise requirements for scalability, security, privacy, reliability, explainability, and operational readiness. By facilitating architecture reviews, resolving cross-team dependencies, and guiding engineering teams through implementation trade-offs, I help organizations move from AI experimentation to production-grade solutions that deliver sustainable business outcomes.
