# Final interview positioning

> Part of Expectation 1: Lead architecture for strategic AI initiatives across products, platforms, and shared enterprise capabilities

The key interview positioning: Present yourself as an architect who can move beyond designing individual AI solutions to establishing enterprise-wide AI capabilities, architecture standards, and strategic roadmaps that enable multiple products and business units to adopt AI successfully.
