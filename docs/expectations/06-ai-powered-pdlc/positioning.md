# 11. Your interview-ready closing statement

> Part of Expectation 6: Help engineering teams leverage AI-powered PDLC capabilities across coding, testing, documentation, analysis, and delivery workflows

My approach would be to transform the traditional software development lifecycle into an AI-enabled engineering lifecycle, embedding AI capabilities across requirements analysis, architecture, coding, testing, documentation, delivery, and production support.

I would start by identifying the engineering bottlenecks and establishing an enterprise AI engineering strategy. This would include approved coding assistants, architecture and requirements copilots, AI-powered testing, engineering knowledge assistants, and AI-enabled DevOps capabilities.

From an architecture perspective, I would focus on integrating these capabilities with existing engineering platforms, source-code repositories, work-item management, test automation, and CI/CD pipelines. I would also establish reusable engineering patterns, secure coding guidelines, agent permissions, and quality gates to ensure that AI-generated artefacts comply with enterprise standards.

I would work with engineering leaders to introduce these capabilities through controlled pilots, measure their impact on productivity, quality, and delivery performance, and then scale successful patterns across teams.

My objective would be to improve engineering effectiveness and delivery speed while maintaining architectural consistency, software quality, security, and long-term maintainability.

## 12. How to position your existing application architecture and Appian experience

There is a useful connection between your application architecture background and this expectation.

You can position your experience around three capabilities:

| **Existing architecture strength** | **How to extend it into AI-powered PDLC** |
| --- | --- |
| Reusable architecture patterns | Create reusable AI engineering templates, prompts, coding standards, and agent configurations |
| BPM and workflow automation | Automate engineering workflows such as approvals, defect management, testing, and release processes |
| Enterprise architecture governance | Establish AI coding standards, architecture compliance checks, security controls, and engineering quality gates |
| Application integration | Integrate AI assistants with APIs, repositories, test platforms, and delivery systems |
| Technology leadership | Drive adoption, develop engineering capabilities, and measure transformation outcomes |

A particularly useful distinction to explain in the interview is:

Traditional automation executes predefined development and delivery steps. AI-assisted engineering helps developers and architects understand context, generate solutions, analyse problems, and make recommendations. Agentic engineering goes further by allowing authorised agents to execute multi-step engineering tasks, subject to permissions and review controls.

Your architectural role is to bring these three approaches together into a governed engineering ecosystem.
