# 6. Your Principal Architect decision-making framework

> Part of Expectation 8: Participate in architecture reviews, technical strategy discussions, and investment decisions related to AI and platform modernization

You should internalise one consistent approach that you can use across architecture reviews, technology strategy and investment discussions.

I recommend the following seven-step framework.

#### The Principal Architect decision framework

1. Understand the business problem

Identify business outcomes, stakeholders, constraints and success measures.

1. Assess the current architecture

Identify existing platforms, reusable capabilities, dependencies, risks and technology debt.

1. Define architectural options

Develop realistic alternatives, including reuse, managed services, custom build and phased modernisation.

1. Evaluate technical and business trade-offs

Assess NFRs, security, scalability, resilience, complexity, TCO and strategic flexibility.

1. Make a recommendation

Explain the selected option and why alternatives are less suitable.

1. Obtain governance and investment decisions

Document decisions, risks, exceptions, funding and accountable owners.

1. Measure implementation and business outcomes

Validate that the decision delivered the expected benefits and revise the architecture when evidence warrants it.

## 7. A reusable architecture decision template

Use this structure when preparing to present a recommendation to an architecture board or technology investment committee.

#### Architecture decision brief

#### 1. Business problem

Introduce an enterprise AI platform for multiple business units

#### 2. Recommended architectural option

Federated platform using approved managed AI services

#### 3. Expected business and technical benefits

Reuse common capabilities, reduce duplication and accelerate onboarding

#### 4. Key risks and trade-offs

Platform complexity, provider dependency and operating-model maturity

#### 5. Decision requested

Approve a phased platform foundation, subject to security, cost and operational validation
