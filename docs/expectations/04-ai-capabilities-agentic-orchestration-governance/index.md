# Expectation 4: Provide architectural leadership for capabilities, agentic workflows, AI orchestration, evaluations, AI observability, and AI governance

This expectation is about leading the architecture of an enterprise AI ecosystem—not simply designing individual LLM applications. A Principal Architect must define how reusable AI capabilities are built and exposed, how AI agents interact with enterprise systems, how multi-step workflows are orchestrated, how AI quality is evaluated, how production behaviour is monitored, and how governance is embedded throughout the AI lifecycle.

The key is to establish an architecture in which AI solutions can be developed and scaled consistently across business units, while remaining secure, reliable, explainable, cost-effective, and aligned with business outcomes. I would approach this by defining enterprise reference architectures, reusable services, orchestration patterns, evaluation frameworks, observability standards, and governance controls. I would also work closely with business, product, data, security, platform, and engineering teams to ensure that AI capabilities move from experimentation into production with measurable value and controlled risk.
