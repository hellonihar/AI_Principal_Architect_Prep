# 4. Suggested interview preparation exercise

> Part of Expectation 1: Lead architecture for strategic AI initiatives across products, platforms, and shared enterprise capabilities

Prepare one architecture case study that you can use across multiple interview questions.

#### Scenario: A large bank wants to establish an enterprise AI platform supporting customer onboarding, cross-selling, loan underwriting, and customer service.

Be prepared to explain:

1. Business objectives and strategic alignment.
1. Current-state architecture and challenges.
1. Target-state enterprise AI architecture.
1. Shared AI capabilities and product-specific components.
1. Integration with existing CRM, BPM, and core banking systems.
1. AI governance, security, and compliance.
1. Architecture roadmap and implementation phases.
1. Business KPIs and expected outcomes.

This scenario is particularly useful because it connects enterprise architecture, AI platform strategy, intelligent automation, integration architecture, and business transformation.
