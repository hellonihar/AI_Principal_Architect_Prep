# 5. Final interview positioning

> Part of Expectation 2: Partner with business units, product leaders, and engineering teams to shape scalable, secure, and practical AI solution architectures

A strong way to summarize this expectation during an interview is:

I see the AI Principal Architect role as a bridge between business strategy, product innovation, and engineering execution. My approach is to work collaboratively with business units and product leaders to identify high-value AI opportunities, translate them into practical architecture designs, and partner with engineering teams to deliver scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions. I focus on building reusable enterprise AI capabilities while respecting business-specific requirements, existing technology investments, and operational constraints. Ultimately, my responsibility is to ensure that AI architecture enables measurable business outcomes while maintaining enterprise standards for security, reliability, governance, and long-term sustainability.
