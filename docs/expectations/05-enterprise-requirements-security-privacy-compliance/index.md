# Expectation 5: Ensure AI solutions align with enterprise requirements for security, privacy, compliance, resiliency, observability, and maintainability

This expectation assesses whether you can design AI solutions that meet the same enterprise-grade architectural standards as mission-critical business applications. It is not sufficient to demonstrate that an AI solution works or produces accurate responses. A Principal AI Architect must ensure that the solution protects enterprise data, enforces identity and access controls, meets regulatory obligations, remains available under failure conditions, provides operational transparency, and can be maintained and evolved over time.

My approach would be to establish a set of enterprise non-functional requirements (NFRs), architecture principles, reference patterns, and technical controls that apply across the AI lifecycle—from data ingestion and model selection through application development, deployment, and production operations. I would work with security, privacy, legal, compliance, infrastructure, data, and engineering teams to translate enterprise policies into enforceable architecture controls. I would also ensure that these controls are measurable through security testing, compliance evidence, resilience testing, observability dashboards, and operational service-level objectives.
